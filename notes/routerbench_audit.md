## What this audit changes — read this first

Hand-written on 2026-10-19 after checking model responses directly, not just score columns.

1. **Change the small model.** `mistral-7b-chat` answers **"A" on 94% of MMLU, 100% of HellaSwag and
   86% of ARC** questions. Its multiple-choice scores (0.25, 0.26, 0.39) are a formatting failure at
   chance level, not a measure of difficulty. A router trained on that pair would learn "multiple
   choice → GPT-4", which is trivially true and says nothing about query difficulty.
   **Proposed pair: S = `mixtral-8x7b-chat`, L = `gpt-4-1106-preview`.** Mixtral parses on 95% of MMLU
   with an even spread of answer letters, its headroom against GPT-4 is ≥ 0.05 on every main dataset,
   and it is the weak model RouteLLM used, so results compare directly with published work. Keep
   Mistral-7B only as a second pair on the free-text datasets (MBPP, MT-Bench, re-graded GSM8K).
2. **Re-grade GSM8K.** Its scores are not correctness. They take five values (0, .25, .5, .75, 1),
   GPT-4 reaches 1.0 on **0%** of items, and its mean is 0.66 where GPT-4's real GSM8K accuracy is
   around 0.9. The responses are all present, so `graders.py` re-scores them by numeric exact match
   against the reference answers.
3. **GSM8K here is the train split, not test.** 7,450 items labelled `dev`, indices up to 7,472. GSM8K
   train has 7,473; test has 1,319. Track B aligns on the train split by question text.
4. **MMLU is the full test set, despite the `val` label.** 14,042 items over 57 subjects, with
   per-subject counts matching the test set exactly (professional_law 1,534, high_school_psychology
   545, abstract_algebra 100).
5. **Prompts and responses are stored as stringified Python lists** (`"['...']"`). The loader must
   parse them with `ast.literal_eval` before any feature sees the text, or TF-IDF will learn brackets
   and escaped newlines.
6. **Drop 21 small custom tasks** (1,719 rows: the Chinese-language tasks, accounting_audit,
   bias_detection, consensus_summary, abstract2title, test-match). They are not in the paper, most
   have under 400 items, and several have broken or negative headroom.
7. **MT-Bench is too small to carry a guarantee.** 80 items cannot supply `n_cal ≥ 1,000` (DESIGN §3.4).
   Report it as a point estimate with a CI. The bounded claims rest on MMLU (14k), HellaSwag (10k) and
   GSM8K (7.4k).
8. **There is a free oracle column**, `oracle_model_to_route_to` (including `no_model_correct`). Use it
   as a cross-check for C's oracle curve, not as a feature.
